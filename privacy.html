Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

"3AM Tales Uploader" is a private tool used only by the owner of the 3AM Tales channels to publish their own videos to their own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

What data the app uses

  • It uses the YouTube permission youtube.upload only to upload videos made by the channel owner to the channel owner's own YouTube channel.
  • It does not access, collect, store, sell or share any data of other users or viewers.
  • The access token is stored only as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub settings and is used only to upload videos.

YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: contact the channel owner through the 3AM Tales Instagram page.
